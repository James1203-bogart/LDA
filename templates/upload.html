Paste the URL of your Google Sheets spreadsheet (the one linked to your Google Forms responses). The spreadsheet must be shared as "Anyone with the link" can view.

How to get the URL
  1. Open your Google Form → click Responses tab.
  2. Click the Google Sheets icon to view responses in Sheets.
  3. In Google Sheets, click Share → set "Anyone with the link" to Viewer.
  4. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar and paste it above.
Tips
  • Include at least one column with open-ended text feedback.
  • Best results with 50 + feedback entries.
  • Maximum file size: 300MB.
